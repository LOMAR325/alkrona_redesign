About us

We grow plants like a lab.
We ship them like a florist.

Lumen Botanica began in 2016 as four racks of grow lights in a disused cold-storage unit on the Merwehaven. The question was simple: if we could measure everything a plant experiences before it reaches a customer, could we stop selling plants that quietly die in week three?

Nine years later it is 2,400 m² of vertical greenhouse, 520 species, and a sensor network that logs light, humidity and substrate moisture every ninety seconds. Every plant ships with its own grow log. Nothing here has been flown in on a pallet.

Closed-loop water

Irrigation runoff is filtered, UV-treated and returned. We use 91% less water per plant than field nurseries.

Zero plastic out

Moulded pulp crates, mycelium padding, paper tape. Nothing in the box needs a recycling bin.

Peat-free substrate

Bark, pumice, coco coir and worm castings — mixed on site, never stripped from a bog.

Powered by the roof

1.1 MW of rooftop solar covers 78% of the LED load. The rest is certified wind.
